# Hop 1: The Joint Keepers Regulations

A new law applies to the Register livestock service: the Livestock Keeping (Joint Keepers) Regulations 2026. It is described in full in docs/joint-keepers-law.md, Part A for the law and Part B for what the service must do.

Change the legacy app in legacy/RegisterLivestock so it complies. Follow Part B exactly: the migration script and its fixed date, the screen changes, the IDs of screen elements, and the wording of every message.

This is a change to legacy code, not a modernisation. Keep the app's existing style and structure, and change only what the law requires.
